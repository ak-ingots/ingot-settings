Changed the layout to that of `ingots.defaults`, a unit in the `__init__.py` of its package, and prefixed the classes, so that an application imports them under their own names: `ingot_settings.SettingsProject`, `ingot_settings.settings.SourcesSettings` and `ingot_settings.settings.loaders.SettingsFileLoader` replace `Project`, `Settings` and `FileLoader`, which the package root no longer exports.
